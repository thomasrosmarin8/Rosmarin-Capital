Purpose

My Mission

Rosmarin Capital is my life's work. I built it around a single belief: that everyone, regardless of where they come from, what they earn, or what they've been told about their potential, deserves a genuine shot at financial freedom. Not because financial freedom is an end in itself, but because of what it unlocks. It is the foundation on which a life well-lived is built.

Part of what drives me is a story I've carried for a long time. The Pursuit of Happyness, and the real man behind it, Chris Gardner. What stayed with me wasn't the ending. It was the people who never get a version of it. Not for lack of will, but because the tools and the entry point were never made visible to them. The financial system has a door, and for too many people, that door is invisible.

I am committed to proving that a firm can be built differently. One that holds itself to the highest standards of investment discipline while being genuinely accessible to all. Everything here is built with full transparency and documented conviction. Whoever you are and wherever you're starting from, I'll work every day to be worth your trust.

The Founder

About Me

I'm Thomas Rosmarin, a 19 year old Finance major at Marquette University, originally from Grand Rapids. I started investing at 15 and fell in love with markets not for the money, but because it was genuinely fun. The stock market clicked with me unlike anything I had ever pursued, and I became obsessed with learning everything there is to know.

At Marquette I carry a 3.9 GPA and have been on the Dean's List every semester. I also help manage two endowment funds as an analyst for the Investment Club and as a member of the Applied Investment Management Portfolio Club, with the funds nearing a combined AUM of $1.5 million.

Outside of finance I'm into pretty much every sport imaginable. I played baseball and tennis in high school, captaining the tennis team and finishing First Team All-State. Though I am a very active person, I also have a deep passion to learn new things.

I love meeting new people and sharing what I know about finance with anyone it can actually help. I hope the work I put into Rosmarin Capital reaches as many people as possible and makes a real difference.
