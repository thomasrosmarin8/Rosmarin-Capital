Investment Philosophy

Our Investment Thesis

🧠

AI & Technology Infrastructure

Roughly half the portfolio is anchored in companies building or benefiting from the AI stack — GOOG, META, and MU. AI is not a trend; it is a platform shift that will compound for decades. We want exposure at every layer: software, semiconductors, and cloud.

⚛

Nuclear Energy Renaissance

AI data centers require enormous, reliable baseload power that wind and solar cannot provide. Nuclear is the only clean answer at scale. Centrus Energy (LEU) is one of the only US-licensed producers of the advanced fuel next-generation reactors need — a rare and strategic asset.

✈

Defense & National Security

Geopolitical complexity is not going away. RTX's Pratt & Whitney engines and defense systems sit on a massive, multi-year backlog. NATO allies rearming and domestic modernization programs make this a structural, not cyclical, tailwind.

🌏

Real Asset Infrastructure

Brookfield Infrastructure (BIP) and Limbach (LMB) provide exposure to essential, hard-to-replicate physical assets — from global utilities and ports to specialized mechanical services. Both benefit from the data center construction wave and offer inflation-protected cash flows.

🚗

Platform Economy

Uber has built the dominant global mobility network with compounding network effects. Its pivot toward profitability is underway, and its partnerships with autonomous vehicle developers position it to benefit from the AV transition rather than be disrupted by it.

✦

Independent, Bottom-Up Research

Every position in this portfolio is built from primary sources — 10-Ks, earnings transcripts, industry data — not sell-side ratings or social media consensus. We hold through volatility when the thesis is intact, and cut when the facts change.

Purpose

My Mission

Financial literacy is one of the most powerful tools for building long-term wealth — yet it remains inaccessible to most people. The financial industry is full of jargon, gatekeeping, and misaligned incentives that keep everyday investors from making informed decisions.

Rosmarin Capital exists to change that. My goal is to share the research process, thinking frameworks, and investment ideas that drive this portfolio in plain English — no fluff, no hype, no hidden agenda.

Whether you're a student learning to invest for the first time, or an experienced investor looking for a second opinion, I want this to be a resource that helps you think more clearly about markets and money.

The Founder

About Me

I'm Thomas Rosmarin — investor, writer, and founder of Rosmarin Capital. I started this project because I believe that rigorous, independent investment research should be available to everyone, not just those with access to expensive analysts or institutional resources.

My interest in investing started early, driven by a fascination with how businesses actually create value — what makes one company thrive for decades while another fades. That curiosity led me to study financial markets, business models, and the psychology of long-term wealth building.

Through Rosmarin Capital, I share my live portfolio, my research process, and my thinking on markets in real time. I believe transparency and accountability — putting real capital behind real ideas — is what separates serious investors from commentators.
